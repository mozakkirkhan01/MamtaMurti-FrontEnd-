# 🏥 Eye Hospital ERP System  

This is a **Hospital ERP System** designed specifically for **Eye Hospitals**. It is developed using **Angular** and provides modules for patient management, billing, inventory, prescriptions, surgeries, staff management, and more.  

## 📌 Features  

✅ **Admin Panel**  
- Admin login & dashboard  
- Role & permission management  
- Change password  

✅ **Patient Management**  
- OPD booking & patient list  
- Prescription & discharge summary  
- Surgery management & consent forms  

✅ **Billing & Finance**  
- OPD, Surgery, Optical & Canteen billing  
- Package bills & collections  
- Daily & monthly billing reports  

✅ **Pharmacy Management**  
- Medicine stock, purchase, sale, and return  
- Expiry medicine tracking  
- Purchase & sale reports  

✅ **Masters**  
- Doctor, Staff, Department, Designation  
- State, City, Company, Supplier, Manufacturer  
- Role & Menu configuration  

✅ **Other Modules**  
- Canteen management  
- Optical store billing & stock  
- GST & taxation management
